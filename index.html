There are people who pass through your life, and then there are people who quietly become part of it — who make ordinary days feel warmer just by being in them. You are that person for me.

Thank you for the laughter that always arrives at exactly the right moment, for the conversations that stretch far past midnight, and for the way you listen — really listen — as though nothing else in the world matters. You have a rare kind of heart: the kind that makes people feel safe, seen, and a little braver than they were before.

Through every season, every distance, and every version of us — you stayed. That is not a small thing. That is the whole thing.

So I built you a garden that never wilts, because I ran out of real flowers to give you. May this year bring you softness, wild joy, and every good thing you quietly hope for.

P.S. The flowers in here never need water. Just like us.
