To my dearest Amna,

I am writing this little note just because you have been on my mind all day long. Not because anything happened, and not because I needed a reason — you were simply there, in the quiet parts of the day, the way you always are.

Every single time I think about your smile, my heart does a tiny happy dance. I do not know how you do it. One small laugh from you and the whole day rearranges itself into something softer.

I hope you know that loving you is the easiest thing I have ever done. Ordinary hours feel like something worth keeping when you are in them — a cup of tea, a walk home, a song on repeat, all of it stitched through with you.

So here is your garden. Every flower I ever sent you, still hanging, still blooming, exactly the way they will keep on blooming for as long as you let me.

I will always love you.

Every bouquet, still hanging
